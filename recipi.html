☕ Pakistani Chai Recipe

How to make the perfect cup of tea

The Essence of Chai

Pakistani chai is more than just a drink — it is a symbol of hospitality. Every household has its own unique style.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon tea leaves (Chai Patti)
  • 2 teaspoons sugar (or to taste)
  • 2-3 cardamom pods (Elaichi)
  • Ginger — a small piece (optional)

Instructions

  1. Pour water into a pot and place it on the stove.
  2. Once the water is hot, add the tea leaves.
  3. Crush the cardamom and ginger, then add them to the pot.
  4. Let it simmer for 2 minutes on medium flame.
  5. Add milk and mix well.
  6. Add sugar and bring to a boil.
  7. Strain into a cup — enjoy! ☕
